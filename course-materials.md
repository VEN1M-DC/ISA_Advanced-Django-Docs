# Course Materials and Coverage

These study guides cover the three course materials supplied October 7, 2026. Original decks are retained for diagrams, images, instructor wording, and exercises that a prose summary cannot reproduce. Source content is attributed to the linked course materials; ISA SAMIEZADE-YAZD contributed the synthesized notes with AI assistance. The client-server deck includes material adapted from Armando Fox and David Patterson under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0, as stated in the source. Retained slides preserve their credits. The notes are educational and noncommercial.
